EXER Landing Page — Recommended Structure
1. Hero section
EXER — User Experience for Better Digital Health
A short positioning statement.
Primary CTA: Register for the Free Workshop
Secondary CTA: Explore Our Consultancy Services

2. Who is EXER?
This should answer in 10 seconds:
What is EXER? Who do you work with? What do you do?
Suggested positioning:
EXER is a digital health user experience and innovation consultancy helping healthcare organizations, startups, NGOs and health professionals design digital solutions that are useful, intuitive and built around real healthcare needs.
Then introduce your approach:
DISCOVER → VALIDATE → DEFINE → DESIGN → DEPLOY
And a short explanation:
We believe better digital health solutions begin by understanding the people, problems and healthcare environments they are designed for.

3. What We Do
I would keep your services to 3–4 clear categories, rather than listing everything you can possibly do.
Digital Health Product Strategy
Helping organizations transform healthcare problems into viable digital product opportunities.
UX Research & User-Centered Design
Understanding users, workflows and pain points to design better healthcare experiences.
Digital Health Product Development Support
Supporting teams throughout ideation, validation, prototyping and implementation.
UX & Product Consultancy
Independent assessment and recommendations for existing digital health products.
Then:
CTA: Work with EXER

4. Our Approach
This is important because it differentiates EXER from someone simply offering "UX design."
DISCOVER
Understand the healthcare problem, users and context.
VALIDATE
Test assumptions with evidence and real users.
DEFINE
Translate insights into clear product requirements and opportunities.
DESIGN
Create intuitive and user-centered experiences.
DEPLOY
Support implementation, testing and continuous improvement.

5. Featured Project — OHSA
This should be one of the strongest sections.
Don't simply say:
"We are working with OHSA."
Tell the story.
Building Better Healthcare Experiences with AI
EXER is currently contributing to the development and user experience of OHSA, an AI-powered medical imaging solution developed by OMIRAI.
Then:
The challenge
Medical imaging can be difficult to access and interpret in settings where specialized resources are limited.
The opportunity
OHSA uses artificial intelligence to support medical imaging interpretation and improve access to technology-assisted healthcare.
EXER's role
EXER contributes a user-centered perspective to the product, focusing on how healthcare professionals and other users interact with the technology and how the solution can become more intuitive, useful and scalable.
Then a CTA:
Explore the OHSA Project
If you have screenshots, this section should have actual product visuals. This is much more convincing than paragraphs.

Free Workshop Series

A practical introductory workshop series designed for students, young professionals, healthcare professionals and aspiring innovators who want to understand how user experience can improve digital health solutions.

Present your work, connect with innovators and receive your certificate.
Then:
100% Free
[Register for the Workshop]

10. Why EXER?
I wouldn't say "10 years of experience" unless that's actually true for EXER/the team.
Instead, use your cross-disciplinary advantage.
Something like:
Healthcare understanding + product thinking + user experience
EXER sits at the intersection of:
Healthcare × Technology × UX × Innovation
Then explain:
We don't approach healthcare products as technology projects alone. We consider the healthcare problem, the people using the solution, the environment in which it will be used, and the realities of implementation.
That is much stronger positioning for you.

11. Partners / Ecosystem
Once partners have actually agreed, you can have:
Building the Digital Health Ecosystem Together
Logos of organizations participating in the workshop series.
But I would not put partner logos here before obtaining permission.
And I'd distinguish between:
Learning Partners
and
Project Partners / Clients
That prevents someone from looking like they're formally endorsing all of EXER when they are only participating in one workshop.

12. Final CTA
End with two choices.
Have a healthcare problem worth solving?
Work with EXER
Want to learn how digital health products are designed?
Join the Free Workshop
That gives the website two clear conversion routes.
The most important thing I'd change
I would not make the homepage primarily about EXER.
Make it about the problem EXER solves.
The visitor should immediately understand:
"These people understand healthcare, UX and technology—and they can either help me build something or help me learn how to build better."
Then OHSA becomes your proof, the workshop becomes your community entry point, and the consultancy services become your commercial offering.
